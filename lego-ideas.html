LEGO Ideas

Image placeholder: The Ultimate Scrooge McDuck Money Bin

My siblings and I have been working together to create LEGO models that we enter on LEGO Ideas. We have entered a number of product ideas, contest ideas, and activity ideas. We create the builds virtually on BrickLink's Studio app and then render them using the same app. You can check out all of our builds by looking at my profile for the account here: https://ideas.lego.com/profile/LegoTastic2/entries?query=&sort=top. However, you do need a LEGO Ideas account to look at a user's profile on LEGO Ideas so in case you don't have a LEGO Ideas account or don't wish to create one I have included links to all of our product ideas below:

  1. 'The Tuscan Brick Restaurant and Gelateria': https://ideas.lego.com/projects/b68218f9-4589-4c7b-a7f2-a5762241b26f
  2. 'The Sound of Music Functional Music Box': https://ideas.lego.com/projects/bda37a60-563c-4ab3-9de5-4abd25e5aaab
  3. 'The Ultimate Scrooge McDuck Money Bin': https://ideas.lego.com/projects/8128cde6-8332-44c1-8381-06da23f599b0

LEGO Ideas

My siblings and I created LEGO models that we entered on LEGO Ideas. We entered a number of product ideas, contest ideas, and activity ideas - none of the product ideas got enough support though :(

We created the builds virtually on BrickLink's Studio app and then rendered them using the same app. Our builds got removed from LEGO Ideas when they updated the website but you can see some of our builds in more detail in these YouTube videos. Sorry for the bad music - we were little kids when we were making these videos (there's also some really bad LEGO stop motion on our YouTube channel if you're interested haha):

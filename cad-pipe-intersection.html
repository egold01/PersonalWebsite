CAD Pipe Intersection (with Calculus)

This was an extra credit project from my AP Calculus BC class. It combines the use of Onshape CAD software and calculus to find the volume of the perpendicular intersection between pipes with the same radius. For this project, I found the volume of intersection when two pipes were intersected and when three pipes were intersected.

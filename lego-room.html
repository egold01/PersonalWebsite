LEGO Room

Image placeholder: LEGO Room carousel

My siblings and I transformed part of our basement into a LEGO Room to store all of the LEGOs that we collected over the years. We began the transformation by creating a SketchUp drawing of what the room should look like. I created the actual drawing which you can see pictured below.

Image placeholder: SketchUp drawing

We then built the lower of the two tables that you can see on the right side of the drawing (with the help of our dad).

Image placeholder: lower table

Below you can see the LEGO Room under construction...

Image placeholder: room under construction

We attached all of the boxes that the LEGO sets came in to the walls, sorted the pieces, organized the instructions, hung some things from the ceiling, set everything else up on the floor and voila, a complete LEGO world! Pictured below, you can see the completed LEGO Room as it currently exists.
